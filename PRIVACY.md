# AI Opportunity Scout for Claude — Privacy

AI Opportunity Scout’s analysis uses work context the user provides in the current conversation. The Scout MCP backend does not receive raw conversation history, memory summaries, files, or the generated opportunity analysis, and does not keep a separate database of those analyses. The scan tool can receive an optional short focus such as work or career.

## Newsletter signup

Only after you explicitly agree to receive Watch Me AI does the signup tool receive your email address, optional first name, and consent. An email address alone is not consent. The backend sends signup data to Kit for newsletter and subscriber management, with the source AI Opportunity Scout | Claude. The full Opportunity Map unlocks after a successful signup response; email confirmation is a separate step.

## Retention, unsubscribe, and deletion

Signup data is kept in Kit while you are subscribed. Unsubscribing stops emails but leaves an unsubscribed record and history in Kit. To request complete deletion, contact mollie@molliemueller.com. The operator’s existing Scout privacy policy states that a Kit subscriber record will be deleted within 30 days of a verified deletion request.

Hosting providers process technical request and delivery data under the service’s operational settings. This plugin does not install website analytics inside Claude. If you visit molliemueller.com, its website analytics policy applies separately.

## Who receives data

The Scout service is hosted on Vercel. Kit receives consented newsletter signup data. Claude processes conversation context under Anthropic’s terms and your account settings. No raw conversation history or opportunity analysis is sent to Kit.
